Original proposal

The original research boundary原proposal的研究边界

The proposal already focuses on Hainan原课题已经聚焦海南

Place and decisions地点与决策

A case-study village and landscape, with selected farms and field-level variation. Farmers make activity and resource decisions.案例村及其景观、选定农场和田块差异；生产活动和资源配置由农户决策。

Activities and change活动与变化

Cropping systems, livestock and aquaculture; possible agroforestry. Spatial variation, changing conditions, uncertainty and investment matter.种植、畜牧、水产，并可能包含农林复合；关注空间差异、条件变化、不确定性与投资。

PhD1–PhD2 linkPhD1与PhD2协作

A shared case-study area and digital twin. PhD1 designs/tests cropping systems; PhD2 allocates and integrates them, then feeds back resource and driver information.共同案例区和数字孪生。PhD1设计并试验作物系统；PhD2做空间配置和活动整合，再反馈资源与驱动信息。

A wider regional scope would change the sampling and model structure, and needs agreement.扩大区域覆盖将改变抽样和模型结构，需要与JR商定。

Route A · Objective 1

A1. Map variation and define adaptive managementA1. 刻画变异，并建立适应性管理框架

Selected farms in the shared case-study area共享案例区内的选定农场

Research question研究问题

Which differences in fields, seasons and households change the feasible choices for farm adaptation?田块、季节和家庭之间的哪些差异，会改变农业系统可行的适应选择？

Units and evidence单元与证据

Fields within farms; farm households; seasons and shock periods. Link soils, water, microclimate, cropping history, prices and resource availability.农场内田块、农户、季节与冲击时段；连接土壤、水、微气候、种植历史、价格及资源可用量。

Method and output方法与产出

Build a traceable baseline. Agree with farmers what to observe, when to review a plan, and which actions remain possible. Test this observe–assess–adjust–review framework.建立可追溯基线；与农户商定观测内容、复审时点和可采取的行动，并检验“观察—评估—调整—复审”框架。

Output: spatial and seasonal evidence plus an explicit adaptive-management framework.产出既包括空间与季节证据，也包括明确的适应性管理方法框架。

Route A · Objective 2

A2. Build and test an integrated farm modelA2. 建立并检验整合型农场模型

Link feasible activities through shared resources用共同资源连接现实可行的生产活动

Decision unit决策单元

A farm household managing fields and any documented livestock, aquaculture or agroforestry activities.管理田块及有证据支持的畜牧、水产或农林复合活动的农户。

Model structure模型结构

Crop sequences and timing; activity budgets; nutrient, water, feed and labour balances; costs, investment and household constraints.作物序列与时点、活动收支、养分/水/饲料/劳动平衡，以及成本、投资和家庭约束。

Testable question可检验的问题

When does integration improve performance, and when do seasonal bottlenecks or risks limit it? Check component responses and whole-farm balances.活动整合何时改善系统表现，何时受季节瓶颈或风险限制？同时检验组分响应与整场收支。

Activity modules enter only when their local data and system boundaries can be defended.只有本地数据和系统边界有依据，活动模块才进入分析。

Route A · Objective 3

A3. Compare fixed and adaptive resource plansA3. 比较固定与可调整的资源配置方案

Nutrients, water and labour remain central养分、水、劳动仍是核心

Choices and constraints选择与约束

Choose activity areas, sequences, inputs and adjustment options. Respect land, seasonal water and labour, nutrient balances, cash and activity-specific limits.决定活动面积、作物序列、投入和调整选项；满足土地、季节水量/劳力、养分收支、资金及活动特有约束。

Performance and risk表现与风险

Assess all five original performance dimensions. Compare adverse weather and price outcomes alongside average performance.评估原proposal的全部五类表现；同时比较不利天气、价格条件下的结果和平均表现。

Fair comparison公平对照

Compare the observed baseline, a fixed plan and a plan that can adjust. Keep resources, candidate activities and information available at each decision time consistent.比较观察基线、固定计划和可调整计划，保持资源、候选活动及各决策时点可获得的信息一致。

  • Food security粮食安全
  • Resource use efficiency资源利用效率
  • Economic profitability经济盈利性
  • Social acceptability社会可接受性
  • Environmental impact环境影响

Output: feasible trade-offs and the cost, benefit and limits of being able to adapt.产出是可行权衡，以及适应灵活性的成本、收益和局限。

Route A · Feasibility

What Route A needs before modelling方案A建模前需要什么

Start with an audit of datasets, variables and coverage从逐字段的数据审计开始

Collect or verify采集或核实

Farm boundaries and calendars; yields and sales; inputs and costs; peak labour and water access; linked activity and material-flow records.农场边界与日历、产量与销售、投入与成本、农忙季劳动和用水条件，以及关联活动和物料流记录。

Borrow carefully谨慎借用

Literature coefficients can support a first model. Record their location, units, uncertainty and local calibration needs.文献系数可支持初始模型；保留地点、单位、不确定性和本地校准需求。

If key activities remain missing关键活动仍缺失时

Consider partner farms or a few complementary systems with traceable links. A crop-only study would narrow Objective 2 and needs JR’s advice.考虑合作农场或少量补充系统，并保留真实联系。若只做种植，会收窄目标2，需要请JR指导。

Uncalibrated assumptions can explore sensitivity; they cannot demonstrate local feasibility.未经校准的假设可用于敏感性探索，不能证明本地方案可实施。

Route B · Proposed extension

A Hainan-wide design with nested units方案B：嵌套单元的海南省域研究

An exploratory alternative for discussion初步备选，供讨论调整

Province and regions省域与分区

Use Hainan as the study extent. Define defensible agroecological zones and farm types. County or grid summaries describe spatial context.以海南为覆盖范围，建立有依据的农业生态分区和农场类型；县域或栅格汇总描述空间背景。

Representative systems代表性系统

Study sampled farm households within those strata. Retain field sites for mechanism testing and local calibration.在分层中调查代表农户，保留田间样点用于机制检验与本地校准。

Two decision levels两个决策层次

Farmers choose farm plans. Regional actors discuss portfolios, infrastructure and feasible exchanges, with farm constraints preserved.农户选择自己的生产方案；区域参与者讨论系统组合、基础设施与可行交换，仍保留各类农户约束。

Hainan would be a set of connected, diverse systems, rather than one farm-level decision unit.海南应被表示为相互联系而有差异的系统，不能当作一个农场决策单元。

Route B · Objective 1

B1. Map diversity and adapt the frameworkB1. 刻画多样性，并扩展适应性框架

Retain Objective 1; extend its sampling and monitoring保留目标1，扩展抽样与监测设计

Regional question区域研究问题

Where do system types, seasonal risks and adaptation constraints differ across Hainan?海南哪些地区的系统类型、季节风险和适应约束不同？

Evidence at matched scales匹配尺度的证据

Screen public soil, land-use, terrain, weather and statistics. Combine them with stratified farm records and field observations. Check years, units, coverage and map error.筛选公开土壤、土地利用、地形、气象和统计资料，再结合分层农户记录及样点观测；检查年份、单位、覆盖和地图误差。

Adaptive framework适应性框架

Link regional risk signals to farm-level review and feasible responses. Revise typologies and recommendations as observations accumulate.把区域风险信号连接到农户复审与可行应对；随新观测更新类型划分和建议。

Public layers are candidates to assess; farm behaviour and resource flows still need direct evidence.公开图层只是待评估候选；农户行为及资源流仍需要直接证据。

Route B · Objective 2

B2. Connect field, farm and regional modelsB2. 连接田间、农场与区域模型

Test transfers between levels before scaling up先检验跨层迁移，再扩大范围

Field and farm田间与农场

Calibrate crop responses at shared field sites; integrate activities and seasonal resource accounts for representative households.用共享样点校准作物响应；在代表农户中整合活动与季节资源收支。

Regional links区域联系

Connect only evidenced exchanges, such as manure or feed, with distance, handling costs, capacity and receiving-land demand. Preserve imports and exports.只连接有证据的粪肥、饲料等交换，并计入距离、处理成本、容量和接收土地需求，保留进出口边界。

PhD1 partnershipPhD1协作

Keep a shared core case and agreed crop-model interfaces. Use extra sites only where transfer or calibration requires them. Agree any change to the original shared-area arrangement.保留共同核心案例和约定的作物模型接口；在需要迁移检验或校准时增加样点。原共享案例区安排的变化须商定。

Wider livestock statistics cannot replace farm-level integration data.更广的畜牧统计不能替代农场层面的活动整合数据。

Route B · Objective 3

B3. Explore feasible regional portfoliosB3. 探索可行的区域系统组合

Retain all five performance dimensions at farm and regional levels在农户与区域层面保留原五类系统表现

Regional choices区域选择

Explore the mix and location of representative system plans and feasible resource exchanges. Keep farm-level choices explicit.探索代表系统方案的组合、位置及可行资源交换，保留农户层面的选择。

Constraints and objectives约束与目标

Combine household feasibility with local nutrient demand, seasonal water/labour limits, transport capacity and agreed regional safeguards. Report gains and burdens by type and area.把农户可行性与本地养分需求、季节水/劳力、运输容量及商定的区域底线结合，分类型、分区报告收益和负担。

Adaptive comparison适应性对照

Compare current configuration, fixed redesign and staged adjustment under shared climate and market scenarios. Show sensitivity to adoption and transfer costs.在相同气候与市场情景下，比较现状、固定重设计和分阶段调整；检验采用率与转运成本的敏感性。

Outputs would support a regional discussion, with no claim of a single optimum for all farmers.结果用于区域讨论，不宣称存在适用于所有农户的唯一最优解。

Evidence and validation

How I would test either route怎样检验两条路线？

Keep data, model, search and implementation evidence separate区分数据、模型、搜索和实施四类证据

Data and transfer数据与迁移

Audit provenance and units; check maps in the field. Hold out farms, sites or seasons where feasible; report coverage and extrapolation.审计来源和单位，实地核图；条件允许时保留独立农场、样点或季节，报告覆盖范围与外推部分。

Model and uncertainty模型与不确定性

Check balances and response functions. Use local calibration, sensitivity ranges and stress scenarios; compare predictions with independent observations.检查收支和响应函数；做本地校准、敏感性与压力情景，用独立观测检验预测。

Search and practical use搜索与实际使用

Check feasibility and repeated runs. Review plans with farmers and PhD1; test selected components and update after observation. Track costs and unworkable choices.检查约束及重复搜索；与农户、PhD1评议方案，试验选定组分并随观测修订，记录成本与不可实施选择。

If repeated observations are unavailable, claims about adaptation remain scenario-based.没有重复观测时，对适应性的结论应限定为情景分析。
